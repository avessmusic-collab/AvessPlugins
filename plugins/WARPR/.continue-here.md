**Key Decisions / deviations recorded in D1:**
- `WarprSynth` is NOT a `juce::Synthesiser` subclass (the base class takes an internal CriticalSection; its voice bookkeeping is bypassed by Poly/Mono/Legato + ghost slots). Block splitting at MIDI timestamps and raw-byte MIDI parsing are implemented in `WarprSynth::renderBlock`. `WarprSound` is not a separate class (voices hold the `SampleData` reference directly).
- Voices own an explicit `SampleData` reference and hand it to the `RetireList` when they end (backlog of 128 if the list is full) - the architecture's "retire list holds an extra reference" alone cannot prevent a last-release on the audio thread.
- Mip blend: lower level fully weighted only up to rho = 1; full upper level from half an octave on (`w = min(1, 2*frac(log2 rho))`) - a refinement of the plain linear blend to limit aliasing leakage.
- Sinc8 is an INTERPOLATING Kaiser sinc (beta 5, cutoff Nyquist) so integer positions are bit-exact; its stopband is not claimed to be 80 dB (band-limiting is the mip pyramid's job). Unmeasured; revisit in D10 if listening shows imaging.
- Seam crossfade law is correlation-weighted (equal-gain for correlated material, equal-power for uncorrelated) and is skipped for the full periodic single-cycle wave or a "clean" seam.
- Mono (non-legato) retrigger keeps the voice and re-attacks the envelope from the current level; One-Shot loop mode instead restarts through a ghost fade.
- Unison pan law is a stereo BALANCE law (unity on the dominant side, cos(|pan| pi/2) on the other): a mono source at centre is unity in both channels (L == R exactly). Unison gain is 1/sqrt(N) with no extra compensation.
- Decimator design: `designIIRLowpassHalfBandPolyphaseAllpassMethod(0.10, -105 dB)` for every stage.
- Velocity law 0.25 + 0.75 v (A2); mod wheel stored, no destination.
- State: the sample blob stays empty until D9 (`SampleStore::latest()` is the encode hook). `playMode` stays Classic-only (Granular is D7).
- The test harness stands in for the processor's 100 ms message-thread RetireList drain timer (`Runner::process` drains every 8 blocks).
